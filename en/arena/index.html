How to start

From login to fight — three minutes

Connect to the serverV Rising → Play → Online Play → Find Servers → “Direct Connect” → 185.207.214.9:27800.
Create a character — you are already in the arenaNo coffin: a couple of seconds after creation you stand in the arena wearing a full set of gear.
Make a build in the ForgeGear, spells, passives, weapons, coatings, blood, elixir — on the website, with hints and numbers.
Paste the code into chatThe Forge gives you one line. Paste it into the game chat — and your fighter is re-geared. The build is saved on the server and comes back next time you log in.

What is in the starter kit

  • 17 T08 weapon types — three artifacts (Cloud Dancers, The Thousand Storms, The Endbringers) and legendaries of every type.
  • Dracula's T09 armor — four sets: Scholar, Rogue, Warrior, Brute.
  • 6 amulets — one per school of magic, the Phantom cloak, a bag.
  • Coatings for all 6 schools and 8 elixirs — infinite.

Lost your items? Type .kit — the kit is given again.

Build

The Forge — fighter editor

Everything that takes hours to assemble in the game takes a minute here. The website knows nothing about the server: it only packs your choices into one line for chat.

One line — the whole fighter

Spells with jewels, ultimate, 5 passives, 8 weapons with stats, blood, armor set, a coating on every weapon and an elixir. The mod puts weapons into slots in your order.

Mistake-proof

You can't pick more than three artifacts; a weapon gets exactly three stats, a jewel — up to four. The Forge tells you if something is empty or doesn't add up.

V Arena codes work

Have a build from V Arena? Paste its code (78 characters) — the mod converts it into its own build and tells you what it understood. Check the result: it's a foreign format.

Blood works for real

The mod gives real blood buffs at quality thresholds 0/30/60/90 and the fifth effect at 100%. You can mix two bloods — one stat is taken from the second.

Nothing falls off

Passives are checked every 2 seconds and restored if the game removed them. A drunk elixir is refilled after a reset and after death. Coatings are infinite.

Reset and forms

A reset (.r, the forms wheel or death) clears buffs and the coating cooldown. Beast forms switch on instantly, and you are invulnerable in beast form.

Modes

Where to fight

Free fight open

PvP in the arena is always on. Teleport points: 1 — arena, 2 and afk. For a warm-up — four training dummies with 25,000 health.

Castle raid arena beta

Two statues — attackers and defenders. Sign up and the mod sorts players into clans, moves them to the castle, gives buffs, potions and siege items to the attackers. Match time — 10 to 30 minutes; the defender's servants are placed from a set made in the Forge.

The match is still being polished: rough edges are possible.

1v1 duels soon

A fight to one death in a shrinking circle, rating (start 1000, +25 for a win, −20 for a loss) and a leaderboard. Switched off for now — we'll turn them on with an announcement in Discord.

Commands

Arena commands

Typed into the game chat, starting with a dot. Start typing — the list filters itself.

Gear and build

.build [code]also .bd
No code — show your build. With a code from the Forge — apply everything at once: spells, jewels, weapons, blood, passives, coatings, elixir.
.import <V Arena code>also .va, .import-build
Bring over a build from V Arena (a 78-character code). The mod tells you what it understood — check the result.
.kitalso .set
Clean starter kit: full re-gear, removes the build, coatings and elixir.
.legendary <weapon> <infusion> <stats>also .l · .l ? — help
A legendary weapon with the stats you want, always at full power. Example: .l axe blood 574.
.artifact <artifact> <stats>also .art · .art ? — help
An artifact (golden) weapon. Example: .art gravecaller 2AC.
.jewel <spell> <stats>also .j · .j <spell> ? — list of stats
A jewel for a spell with the stats you choose.
.blood <blood> [second] [quality] [effect]also .b
Change your blood; a mix of two is possible: one stat (1–3) is taken from the second. Quality defaults to 100.
.bloodpotion <blood> [quality] [amount]also .bp
Blood potions (Bloodwine) with the blood and quality you want.
.resetalso .r
Full health and all cooldowns reset. Works even in combat.

Raids and duels

.raid join attack|defendor just walk up to a statue
Sign up for the raid arena as an attacker or a defender. .raid leave — leave, .raid status — what is going on.
.raid invite <player> <plot> <minutes> <shard>.raid accept / .raid decline
Invite a player to a raid: you attack, they defend their castle. They answer accept or decline.
.raid time <min>.raid loc
Choose the match length (10–30 min) and the location.
.servants [8-character code]also .set-servants
Servants for the coffins of your castle — one character per coffin, 0 — empty. Easiest to make in the Forge, “Castle raid” tab.
.gg
Surrender and end the raid early.
.duel <name> | accept | deny | ffalso .d · soon
1v1 duel: challenge, accept, decline, surrender. Duels are switched off for now.
.topsoon
Duel rating.
.statsalso .st · soon
Your duel statistics.
