General

Fair play

Cheats, macros, third-party tools and bug abuse — ban. Found a bug? Open a ticket in Discord, it’s appreciated.

Respect

Argue about the game, not the person. No insults, harassment, spam or ads for other servers.

Administration

Disputed cases are decided by the admins. Disagree with a decision — open a ticket, not a thread in public chat.

PvP on Matryoshka PVP

What is allowed and what is not

Allowed

  • Attack anyone in an FFA zoneRed areas on the map: the level rule does not apply there.
  • Take revengeOn whoever killed you in the last 15 minutes — regardless of level.
  • Fight backIf a lower-level player attacked you first — defend yourself.
  • Hunt skulls and shardsA skull bearer (10 kills in a row) and a shard carrier are fair game for everyone.
  • LootSearch bodies and enemy chests, take the event horse and carriage loot.

Not allowed

  • Kill the weakA player more than 10 levels below you (gear 80+ — more than 5) — outside the exceptions on the left. The highest level in the clan counts. A violation shows up for everyone as a red line in chat.
  • Use bugs and cheats in combatGetting stuck in terrain, invulnerability through game bugs and the like.
  • Break castles outside the raid windowDamage to castles outside the window does not go through — trying to get around that counts as bug abuse.

The list is being expanded: detailed PvP rules will be published before launch.

Raids and castles

How raids work

Raid window — every day 20:00–22:00 MSK (UTC+3)

First window — Saturday, October 10, 20:00. Outside the window castles take no damage.

Offline protection

If by 20:00 your whole clan has been offline for 15 minutes or more, your castle can’t be raided that evening. Someone from the clan logs in — the protection is gone.

Clans of up to 2, one castle per clan

A third player at someone else’s siege is a spectator: invisible and can’t touch anything.

Castle Heart

Can only be destroyed while decaying. A siege golem spawn is shown to everyone on the map.

Penalties

What happens for violations

Warning → mute → ban

The usual order for chat offences and minor in-game violations.

Instant ban

For cheats, macros and leaking other people’s data.

Breaking the level rule

Shown to everyone as a red line in chat; the admins review every case.
